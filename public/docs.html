01
/schedule Only visible to you

Preview the whole week before you commit

Shows your complete 7-day training split for your current rank — every exercise and every set/rep count, Monday through Sunday, including rest days. Nothing here is logged; it's purely a preview so you know what's coming before you start a session.

  1. Run /schedule
  2. Choose Gym 🏋️ or Calisthenics 🤸
  3. Page through the week with ⬅️ ➡️

Difficulty and volume scale automatically as your rank climbs.

02
/startworkout Only visible to you Logs progress

Today's session, ready to log

Unlike /schedule, this pulls up only today's workout based on the actual day of the week and your current rank, and gives you a button to log it the moment you finish. This is the command that actually moves your Total Workouts count and rank forward.

  1. Run /startworkout
  2. Choose Gym or Calisthenics — rest days show a recovery message instead
  3. Finish the exercises, then tap ✅ Complete Workout

Reach a new milestone and you'll get a level-up announcement on the spot.

03
/myworkout Only visible to you Synced with dashboard

Build your own split from scratch

Opens your private Armory — separate from the built-in Gym/Calisthenics routines, this is where you create fully custom weekly schedules with your own exercises and set/rep counts for each day.

  1. Run /myworkout
  2. New Schedule to name a routine (e.g. "Push Pull Legs")
  3. Pick a day from the dropdown, then Add Exercise to log a movement + sets/reps
  4. Switch between saved schedules anytime with the schedule dropdown
  5. Clear All wipes everything if you want a clean slate

Everything you build here appears instantly on the web dashboard, and vice versa.

04
/resetworkout Destructive Synced with dashboard

A clean slate, if you really want one

Wipes your Total Workouts count and drops your rank all the way back to Level 1: Novice / Beginner. Useful if you're starting a fresh training cycle and want your progress to reflect it honestly.

  1. Run /resetworkout
  2. Read the warning carefully
  3. Confirm with Yes, Reset My Progress, or back out with Cancel

⚠️ This cannot be undone — and it resets your count on the dashboard too.

05
/remindworkout Only visible to you

Never let the arena forget you

Sets a daily ping 5 minutes before your chosen workout time, in your own timezone, sent to a channel of your choice. If you already have one set, you'll be offered Update or Delete instead of stacking a second reminder.

/remindworkout time_range: e.g. 6:00 AM to 7:00 AM

timezone: IST, UTC, EST, CST, MST, PST, GMT/BST, or AEST (defaults to IST)

channel: optional — defaults to the channel you ran the command in

The reminder ping itself includes its own Delete Reminder and How to Update Time buttons.

06
/levels Only visible to you

The Path of Ascendance

Displays the full 10-rank progression system — how many logged workouts (via /startworkout) it takes to reach each stage, roughly mapped to a month of consistent training.

07
/flex Synced with dashboard

Your personal-record hall of fame

A paginated log of specific stats and PRs you record by hand — think "Planche hold: 15 seconds" — separate from your overall workout count. This is your PR journal, not a workout tracker.

  1. Run /flex
  2. Add to log a new exercise/skill + result — logging the same exercise again automatically archives the old entry
  3. Delete a specific numbered entry, or type "all" to clear everything
  4. Menu / Archived to flip between active and retired entries
08
/diet Only visible to you

Fuel matches effort

A browsable reference of common protein sources, sortable by protein content or calorie count, with values shown per 100g so you can plan meals around your training.

  1. Run /diet
  2. Choose Sort by Protein 🥩 or Sort by Calories 🥗
  3. Page through the ranked list with Back / Next
09
/hype Public in channel

No excuses. Let's get to work.

Drops a direct link to the official Gladiator V YouTube Music training playlist — one command, no menus, just something to get locked in before a session.
